summary: "What you will find here, where the data comes from and how often it updates."
---

This monitor brings together **Caracas Stock Exchange** prices, the official and parallel exchange rates, monetary aggregates, inflation, the oil sector, foreign trade, agriculture and economic news on Venezuela.

**How to read the data**

- Series marked as automatic update on their own from the official source.
- Manually loaded series update when the source publishes; their date is always shown.
- **Data & sources** shows the status of every series and when the next data point is expected.

Figures come from public sources and are not investment advice.
